# Privacy Policy

Clinder respects user privacy and handles user data as described below.

## 1. Handling of Clipboard Data

Clinder accesses and stores clipboard information locally on your device in order to provide clipboard history features.

All clipboard data is processed and stored entirely within your device (local environment) and is never transmitted to any external server or shared with third parties.

## 2. Collection and Transmission of Personal Information

Clinder does not collect or transmit any personal information, device identifiers, or access logs to external servers.

## 3. Data Management and Deletion

Users can manage or clear their saved clipboard history at any time directly within the application.

## 4. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this policy, please reach out via:

[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/oktntko/clinder/issues)
